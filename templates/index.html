Composite Index

The Composite Index is the combination of individual indicators which measures the economic cycles behaviour. The advantage of Composite Index compared to individual analysis is the tendency to smooth out some of the volatility of the series. The Composite Index is generally more reliable in generating clear and consistent turning points than individual indicators. There are three types of Composite Index in the Malaysian Economic Indicators, i.e. leading, coincident and lagging indexes.

Leading Index

The Leading Index measures anticipations of the overall economic activity on average of four to six months ahead.

Components

  • Real Money Supply, M1
  • Bursa Malaysia Industrial Index
  • Real Imports of Semi Conductors
  • Real Imports of Other Basic Precious Metals
  • Number of Housing Units Approved
  • Expected Sales Value, Manufacturing
  • Number of New Companies Registered

Coincident Index

The Coincident Index is a comprehensive measure of the overall current economic performance.

Components

  • Total Employment, Manufacturing
  • Real Salaries & Wages, Manufacturing
  • Industrial Production Index
  • Real Contributions, EPF
  • Capacity Utilisation, Manufacturing
  • Volume Index of Retail Trade

Lagging Index

The Lagging Index is to validate the signal of the Leading and Coincident Indexes.

Components

  • Unit Labour Cost, Manufacturing
  • Number of Investment Projects Approved
  • Number of New Vehicles Registered
  • Exports of Natural Gas & Crude oil
  • CPI for Services
